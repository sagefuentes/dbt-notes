## Why check the facts?
- Thoughts and interpretations can set off painful emotions
- Faulty beliefs lead to emotional misery
- Faulty beliefs cause new problems
- Thinking in absolutes (always/never) can set off extreme emotions
	- Event -> Thoughts -> Emotion
- Our emotions can affect what we think about events and how we react to them. Temporary moods/emotions can influence ideas, thoughts, memories, and perceptions
	- Event -> Emotion -> Thoughts
- Believing our thoughts as facts is a recipe for disaster
- There is always more than one way to see a situation
- knowing the facts is essential for solving problems
- examining our thoughts and checking the facts can change our emotions

## How to check the facts?
1. What is the emotion I want to change? (p198) rate the intensity of that emotion from 0-100
2. What is the prompting event?
	- Can be internal or external
3. What are my interpretations? (thoughts, beliefs, assumptions, judgments)
	- Think of other possible interpretations
		- For me, interpretations that are not further activating
	- Practice looking at all sides or all points of view
4. Am I assuming a threat/feared outcome?
	- Label threats, asses the probability that they are real/will occur (wise mind)
5. What is the catastrophe?
	- Imagine catastrophe happening and coping well with it 1 day? 1 week? 1 month?
6. Does my emotion and/or intensity fit the facts?
