## What is validation and why is it important?
- Validation is acknowledging and seeing the truth from another person's perspective or position. Verifying their facts and truths in the situation.
- Acknowledging emotions, thoughts, and behaviors of another to the point of understanding.
- It is NOT agreeing with them (you may agree with them, you may not)
- It is NOT saying something from their perspective or position is the ultimate truth (we all have bias in our perspectives and something can be much more messy and complicated than that, someone's emotions can be valid but their actions ones we disagree with)
- It is NOT validating the invalid

## "How to" guide to Validation
- Pay attention
	- Engage your body fully.
	- Actively listen
	- No multitasking (caveats)
	- Stay present, direct your focus
	- Use non-verbal communication
- Reflect Back
	- Communicate back that you understand.
	- NO judgmental language or voice tone
		- Might need [[STOP Skill]]
- "Read Minds" or be mindful of nonverbal cues
	- Be sensitive to the things not being said or expressed.
	- Pay attention! Body language, facial experienc
	- Acknowledge limits, some people don't read each other's nonverbal cues well, so many complicated factors
- Understand
- Acknowledge the Valid
- Show Equality

## Traumatic invalidation
(loosely p164-165)
- Repeated and pervasive invalidation by important people in our lives
	- Not necessarily people we know, could be actors we have looked up to all our lives, politicians that impact our livelihoods, etc.
- Can also come about from MAJOR one time occurrences
Long lasting impacts:
- Difficulty labeling and regulating emotions
- Self-invalidation
- Diminished [[States of Mind#Wise Mind|wise mind]]

## Validate yourself
- Validate yourself exactly the way you would validate someone else
- How can I validate myself?
	- Use mindfulness skills (consider [[How Skills]], [[What Skills]], [[Mindfulness of Emotion]], [[Mindfulness of Current Thoughts]])
	- Use [[Radical Acceptance]]
	- Change how I respond to myself
