Behavioral modification is a portion of the larger discipline of behavioralism.

|                                           | Reinforcement                                                                                                                                       | Punishment                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ----------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Positive <br>(increasing the presence of) | Increases behavior by increasing the presence of a stimulus.<br>Example: You get a treat after you study, so you study more.                        | Decreases behavior by increasing the presence of a stimulus.<br>Example: You get reprimanded each time you punch a wall, so you punch walls less.                                                         |
| Negative<br>(decreasing the presence of)  | Increases behavior by decreasing the presence of a stimulus.<br>Example: You play with your toys more after you clean them, so you clean them more. | Decreases behavior by decreasing the presence of a stimulus.<br>Example: You get additional tasks assigned to you when you complete tasks incorrectly, so you decrease how often you do them incorrectly. |
