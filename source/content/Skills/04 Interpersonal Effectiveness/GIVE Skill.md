Meant to be stacked with [[DEAR MAN Skill]] and [[FAST Skill]]

## (Be) Gentle
- Be nice and respectful
	- Ask if they have bandwidth in the moment to ask 
	- Be mindful of known factors on the person
	- Be present, mindful, and willing
- No attacks
	- No verbal or physical attacks. 
	- Be mindful of hostile body language.
	- No harassment of any kind. 
	- Express anger directly with words (not directed at the person).
- No judging
	- No moralizing.
	- No "If you were a good person,..."
	- No shoulds.
	- Abandon blame.
- No sneering
	- No smirking, eye rolling, sucking teeth.
	- No cutting off or walking away.
	- No insults of the idea, no diminishing of the idea, no "I don't care"
## (Act) Interested
- Listen and appear interested in the other person.
- Listen to the other person fully, active listening.
- Face the person; maintain eye contact; lean toward the person rather than away.
- Don't interrupt or talk over the person.
- Be sensitive to the other person's wish to have the discussion later.
- Be patient.
## Validate
- With words and more importantly, actions, show that you understand the other person.
- Try and see the world from the other person's perspective.
- NOT cheerleading, NOT reinforcement, NOT enabling.
- Levels
	1. Pay attention: Look interested, no phubbing
	2. Reflect back: say back what you heard in your own words and check your understanding. No judgment!
	3. Being sensitive to the unspoken: Be sensitive to nonverbals or known factors, read between the lines
	4. Understand: look for how they feel, think, or are doing make sense
	5. Acknowledge the valid
	6. Show equality
## (Use an) Easy manner
- Have some ease to the best of your ability
- Use a little humor
- Smile!
- Be lighthearted
- Use "soft sell" over "hard sell"
	- Gentle, low-pressure
- Leave your attitude at the door
