Useful to stack with [[GIVE Skill]], [[FAST Skill]], and [[Modulating Intensity]]

When to use DEAR MAN
- asking for a change in relationship
- what you want or need from other people
- how you want to be treated
- ask for your rights to be respected
- ask that you opinions to be taken seriously 

## Describe
- Describe the current situation (if needed). Stick to the facts.
- Tell the person exactly what you are reacting to.
- Shorter might be better here, be concise.
## Express
- Expression yourself, communicate your feelings and opinions about the situation.
- Don't assume someone else knows how you feel.
- Try and steer away from shoulds.
- Try and stick with "I" statements.
## Assert
- Ask really clearly what you want or deny the request by saying no clearly.
- Do not assume that others will know what you want without clearly stating.
- Remember that no one is a mind reader.
## Reinforce
- Have consequences outlined.
- Reinforce/reward the person ahead of time (if you can) by explaining positive effects of getting what you want or need.
- If necessary, also clarify the negative consequences of not getting what you want or need
## (Stay) Mindful
- Keep your focus on the goals
- Maintain your position. Don't be distracted. Don't get off topic.
- Broken record
	- Keep asking/saying no/expressing your opinion over and over.
	- Just keep replaying the same thing again and again.
- Ignore attacks
	- If another person attacks, threatens, or tries to change the subject, ignores the threats, comments, or attempts to divert you
	- Do not respond to attacks. Ignore distractions.
	- Just keep making your point
## Appear confident
- Appear effective and competent
- Use a confident voice tone and physical manner; make appropriate eye contact.
- No stammering, whispering, staring at the floor, or retreat.
- No saying, "I'm not sure", or really in my case, "I'm not sure", etc.
## Negotiate
- IF appropriate
- Be willing to give to get.
- Offer and ask for other solutions to the situation or problem.
- Reduce your request (maybe plan a lower expectation, or do the business thing of overasking at the beginning)
- Say no, but offer to do something else or to solve the problem another way.
- Focus on what will work
- Turn the tables
	- Turn the problem over to the the other person
	- Ask them for solutions
