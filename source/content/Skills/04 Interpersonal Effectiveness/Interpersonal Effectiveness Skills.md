## Goals of Interpersonal Effectiveness (p107)
### Be skillful in getting what you want and need from others
- Incentivize other to do what you would like for them to do (without manipulation)
- Incentivize other to consider you opinion
- Allow yourself to say no to unwanted requests

### Build relationships and end destructive ones
- Strengthen current relationships
	- Don't let hurts and problems build up
	- Use relationship skills to head off problems
	- Repair relationships when needed
	- Resolve conflicts before they get too big
- Find and build new relationships
- End hopeless relationships

### Walk the middle path
- Create and maintain balance in relationships
- Balance acceptance and change in relationships
## Factors in the Way of Interpersonal Effectiveness (p109)
- You don't have the interpersonal skill you need
- You don't know what you want
	- You are skilled, but can't figure out what you really want or need from someone else.
	- You are having trouble balancing the needs for both people
		- Ask for too much versus not asking for anything.
		- Always saying yes or always saying no, regardless of actual wants.
- Your emotions are getting in the way
	- You have the skills, but your emotions are an obstacle to those skills (meaning we likely need distress tolerance skills first).
- You forget your long-term goals for short-term goals
	- You put your in the moment urges and desires ahead of your long-term goals. The future no longer is a factor in how you act in the moment.
- Other people are getting in your way
	- You have the skills, but other people outside of the dyad are presenting obstacles for you.
	- Other people have more (legitimate) power than you do.
	- Other people may find you hostile or dislike you or may not want you to get what you want.
	- Other people may not want you to reach your goals without a cost such as your self-respect.
- Your thoughts and beliefs are getting in the way
	- Fear about negative consequences if you ask for what you want or deny a request.
	- Belief that you are undeserving of what you want makes you stop.
	- Belief that others are undeserving of their wants makes us ineffective.


## Addressing Miscommunication 
(from a TED Education video)
- Engage actively (active listening vs. passive hearing)
- Listen with eyes, ears, full body
- Take time to understand as you are trying to be understood
- Be aware of our bias

## Obtaining Objectives Skillfully (p115)
### Clarifying priorities
- How important is:
	- Getting wants and needs meet?
	- Keeping the relationship and connection?
	- Maintaining your self-respect?
Objectives effectiveness
- Getting what you want from someone else.
- Obtaining legitimate rights.
- Getting another person to do something I would like them to do.
- Saying no to requests of unwanted or impossible.
- Getting your opinion or point of view taken seriously.
- Resolving interpersonal conflict.
- [[DEAR MAN Skill]]
Relationship effectiveness
- Act in a way that maintains a positive relationship so that you and the other person feel good about each other.
- Act in a way that others keeps liking and respecting you.
- Balance of short-term and long-term goals.
- Maintaining relationships that matter to you.
- How do I want the other person to feel about me regardless of how the interaction goes (whether or not I have my request granted and whether or not I have granted their request)?
- What do I have to do to get or keep this relationship? Does that align with your values?
- [[GIVE Skill]]
Self-respect effectiveness
- Act in a way that you keep your self-respect.
- Respecting my own values and beliefs.
- Acting in a way that aligns with me ethically/morally.
- Acting in a way that makes you feel capable and effective.
