## Be Fair
- Be fair to yourself and others. 
- Remembering to validate your own and other's feelings and wishes.
	- Reference [[GIVE Skill]] and [[Validation]]
## Don't Over Apologies
- No apologies for existing or asking requests of others.
- No apologies for being alive!
- No apologies for having your own viewpoint or differing viewpoints.
- Reminders to keep a posture that is not one of ashamedness
	- No eyes and head down 
	- No body slumped. 
- No invalidating the valid.
## Stick to Your Values
- Don't sell yourself out! (unless it is for something actually important)
- Know your values! Be clear on what your values and ideals are and how they impact the situation.
	- If you don't consider making a list or holding off on decisions until you have time to consider your values again.
## Be Truthful
- Don't lie or exaggerate. 
- No excuses. 
- No faked helplessness.
- No need to embellish or be hyperbolic.
- Not being truthful is undervaluing of your current state and potentially damaging to your self-respect
