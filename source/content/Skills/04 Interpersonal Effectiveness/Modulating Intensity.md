## Factors to consider for intensity
How intense should some interpersonal interaction be? Consider having already gone through a [[DEAR MAN Skill|DEAR MAN]] or through [[FAST Skill|FAST]], how intense should a request or a denial be?
Factors
1. Capability
	- Can this person even help or facilitate with this request?
	- If they can, how easily can they help?
2. Priorities
	- What is important here? A goal, a relationship, yourself?
	- If the relationship is the most important thing, consider reducing intensity.
	- If your goal or yourself is the most important thing, consider increasing intensity
3. Self-respect
4. Rights
5. Authority
6. Relationship
7. Long-term vs. short-term goals
	- Are your long-term or short-term goals more important?
8. Give and take/reciprocity
	- What is the give and take in the relationship?
9. Context/Homework
	- Do you have the appropriate context for the request?
10. Timing
	- Is this the right time for this request?
