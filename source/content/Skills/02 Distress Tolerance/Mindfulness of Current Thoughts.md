1. Observe your thoughts
	- As waves, coming and going
	- No suppression
	- No judgment
	- Acknowledge the presence of the thoughts
	- Not keeping thoughts around
	- Not analyzing thoughts
	- Practice willingness in these moments
	- Stepping back and observing thoughts as the pass into and out of your mind.
2. Adopt a curious mind
	- Ask, "Where is this thought coming from?" Watch, wait, find out.
	- Notice that thoughts both come into our mind and also leave.
	- Observe without evaluation or judgement
3. Remember: You are not your thoughts
	- Do not act on all thoughts, some are not necessarily what you want
	- Remember times with different thoughts
	- Remind yourself that catastrophic thinking is emotion mind
	- Remember how your mind is when you are not in a moment of intense pain, suffering, or rumination.
4. Don't block or suppress thoughts
	- Ask, "What am I trying to avoid?" Turn your mind to that sensation. Then return to the thought. Repeat as needed.
	- [[STOP Skill|Stop]], Step back, even physically. Allow thoughts to come and go with your breathe.
	- Adopt a playful stance with your thoughts. 
		- Repeat them loud and fast. 
		- Sing them. 
		- Imagine thoughts as words from a jester
		- Imagine thoughts as recordings getting mangled
		- As cute animals you can cuddle
		- As bright colors in your mind
		- As sounds
	- Try loving your thoughts (if it is a serviceable thought to love)
