## Imagery
- Imagine everything goes well.
- Imagine nature, rivers, clouds, and other pleasant things
## Making Meaning
- Focus on or find meaning or purpose in a painful situation
- Compare to the absurdism
- Repeat these positive aspects in your mind
## Prayer
- Ask for strength to bear the pain
- Turn things over to the universe
## Relaxation
- Take a hot shower
- Drink a calming drink like ice cold water or throat coat
- Meditate
- Willing-Hands
- Half-smiling
- Massage
- Relax your face
## One thing in the moment
- Focus on one aspect or focus entirely on what you are doing
- Keep yourself present
