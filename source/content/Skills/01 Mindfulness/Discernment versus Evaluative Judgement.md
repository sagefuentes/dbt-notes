Frequently in DBT we have been talking about judgment, but some judgment is different than others. Discerning judgment is the type of judgment that describes what something IS so, for example, this water is warm, this water is cold, those are discerning judgments. Evaluative judgment is the type of judgment where a quality is imposed on something, so for example all water is gross.

Discerning judgment: Facts or statuses of something. Statements made without attachment.
- Most rocks are hard
- Liquid water is wet. 
Evaluating judgments: Opinions that infer a quality onto something that does not relate to facts. 
- Rocks are bad.
- Water is gross.

Differences in discerning and evaluating: The above statement "water is gross" is an evaluating statement, but the statement "I dislike water" is a discerning statement. Notice the difference of how one is a fact about yourself and the other is a statement about all water.
