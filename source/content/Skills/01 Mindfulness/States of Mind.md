## Wise Mind 
The state of mind which uses the input from both the Emotion Mind and Logic Mind. It is a more holistic stance for the mind and self, you are considering the entire human system. The focus is on the experience, the here and now.
- [[What Skills]]
- [[How Skills]]

## Emotion Mind 
The state of emotions being in control. Logic is not present. Being in emotion mind can feel overwhelming, confusion, like there is a lack of resolution, or inability to move forward. You are often ruled by moods, feelings, urges, desires, and whims. 
- Emotion mind vulnerabilities are what we address with several skills
Some things make us more vulnerable to emotion mind!
- Sickness
- Tiredness, fatigue, or lack of sleep
- Psychoactive influence
- Stress
- Hungry

Remember, emotions aren't bad by themselves! That is an [[Discernment versus Evaluative Judgement|evaluative judgement]].
## Logic Mind 
The state of logic being in control. Emotions are ignored. Situations are approached intellectually. There is a logical and rational approach to thinking. Cool, calm, collected, and cold.
