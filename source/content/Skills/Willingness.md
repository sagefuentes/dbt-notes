## What is Willingness?
- Accept what is and responding effectively
- Doing what works

Observe it and label it
## Find a willing response to each situation
- Willingness is doing just what is needed
	- In each situation
	- Wholeheartedly, participate fully without dragging
- Willingness is listening very carefully with your [[States of Mind|Wise Mind]] and then acting on that wise advice.
- Willingness is acting with awareness that you are connected to the universe


## Willingness, Step by Step
1. Observe
2. Radically Accept
3. [[Turn Your Mind]]
4. Try [[Half-Smile and Willing Hands]]
5. When willfulness is immovable, ask, "What's the Threat?"

## What is Willfulness?
- Trying to control
- avoid or escape
- Saying no or yes but
- focus on self
- grudge bitterness resentment
