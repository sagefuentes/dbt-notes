Considered an interpersonal effectiveness skill in the book (p152), I also see this as a mindfulness practice
## Core Ideas
- The Universe is filled with opposing sides/forces
- Everything and everyone is connected somehow
	- 'Fun' thought experiment/exercise. Take two things (maybe at random), find a common thread or how one impacts the other. 
- Change is the only constant in the universe
- Change is transactional
	- What we do influences the world around us and has consequences (good, bad, and neutral).
	- What the environment and others do influences the world and has consequences.
	- The world is not alchemical
		- There is not always an equal and opposite reaction for every action
		- A small stimuli could cause a meltdown; a meltdown could cause nothing or little
