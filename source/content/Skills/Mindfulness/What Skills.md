## Observe
- Notice your body
	- Sight, sound, smell, touch, taste
	- Other senses like our position (proprioception), pain (nociception), sense of balance (vestibular sense or equilibrioception), temperature (thermoception), or bodily states like hunger, our heartbeat (interoception)
- Pay attention
	- This can be paying attention to any of our sensations or external sensations/stimuli
- Control your attention
	- Don't be an active actor in your world or body yet, just watch.
	- Do not avoid.
	- Experience the full breadth
- Practice wordless watching
	- Let thoughts and ideas come and go with ease like water in a river or clouds on a blustery day.
- Observe both inside and outside yourself.
## Describe
- Put words on the experience
	- Label and identify the events playing out before you
- Label what you observe
	- Put a label to feelings, 
	- Later acknowledge [[How Skills#Nonjudgmentally|nonjudgmentally]] if the label [[Check the Facts|fits the facts]] or not
- Unglue your interpretations and opinions
- If you can't observe it through your senses, you can't describe it
