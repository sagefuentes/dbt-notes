## Nonjudgmentally
- See, not "good" or "bad"
	- see [[Discernment versus Evaluative Judgement]]
- Not adding our own facts
- Observe how judgments impact our emotions
  Accept holistically
- acknowledge but don't judge
- Don't judge your judging
## One-mindfully
- Concentrate your mind
- Riveting yourself to now
- Doing one thing at a time
	- Notice the desire to be half-present, to be somewhere else, to go somewhere else in your mind, to do something else, to multitask, BUT then choosing to come back to the single thing in the moment.
	- When you are eat, only eat.
	- When you are walking, just walk.
	- When you worry, only worry
	- When you are planning, focus on planning
	- When you are remembering, only remember.
- Let go of distractions
## Effectively
- Be mindful of your goals and the situation
- Focus on what works
- Play by the rules
- Act as skillfully as you can
- Let go of willfulness and sitting on one's own hands

1. Observe when you begin to get angry or hostile. Ask yourself, "is this effective?"
2. Observe yourself when you start wanting to be "right" instead of effective or in line with your values.
3. Notice willfulness in yourself. Ask yourself, "Is this effective?"
4. Drop willfulness, and practice acting effectively instead. Notice the difference.
5. When feeling angry or hostile or like you are about to do something ineffective, practice willing hands.
