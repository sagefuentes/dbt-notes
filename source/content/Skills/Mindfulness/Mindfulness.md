## Goal of Mindfulness
- Reduce suffering and increase happiness
- Increase control of your mind
- Experience reality as it is
	- Remember, truth and facts are not always reality.
	- You can have something be true or factual from your perspective and not have it align with reality. This is not the fault of our own.
- Live life with your eyes open
- Experience the reality of your...
	- Connection to the universe
	- Essential "goodness"
	- Essential validity

## What is Mindfulness
- Intentionally living with awareness in the present moment without judging or rejecting the moment
